Museum Guide

The Cursed Museum is a shared, living collection — every painting, bust, mirror and donated relic carries a record that changes as visitors remember it, challenge it, and vote on which version to believe.

Somewhere in this building, a story is always being rewritten. None of them can be fully trusted; that is the point.

  • Drag, or use Left/Right, to look around a room.
  • Scroll, pinch, or +/- to zoom.
  • Hover or focus a glowing object to see what it is.
  • Click an object to open its archive dossier.
  • Other visitors appear as small coloured ghosts in the room.
  • The floor plan shows which room each visitor currently occupies.
  • Every collection object — built-in or donated — has an evolving record of accounts.
  • Visitors may add testimony, a contradiction, a revision, or counter-evidence.
  • Visitors vote an account Believable, Suspicious, or Definitely Cursed.
  • You may donate a new relic: choose its appearance, gallery, title and origin story.
  • Later visitors can discover your relic and keep expanding its legend.
