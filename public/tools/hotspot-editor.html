Mode
Polygon: click/tap to add vertices, double-click or press Done to finish.
Ellipse: press and drag from the centre outward.
Select/Edit: drag vertices, click an edge-dot to insert, click a vertex then Delete Selected (or press the Delete/Backspace key) to remove — or drag a box over empty space to marquee-select several vertices at once (Delete removes all of them, arrow keys nudge all of them together). Finishing a polygon switches here automatically.
AI-trace: draw a loose box around one object; a real segmentation model (not a colour-threshold guess) finds its outline. Refine the result by hand afterward like any other polygon.
